# Store listing

## Short description

See YouTube videos the way they were uploaded: original titles, descriptions, thumbnails and audio. No auto-translation, no AI dubbing.

## Long description

YouTube localizes what you watch. Titles and descriptions are translated into your interface language, thumbnails are swapped for per-language versions, videos start in a dubbed or AI-dubbed audio track, subtitles switch on by themselves, and an AI summary appears under the video.

restoreyt puts the creator's originals back:

- Original titles everywhere: home, search, subscriptions, the watch page, Shorts, playlists, the player and the browser tab. YouTube's A/B test titles are replaced by the creator's main title.
- Original descriptions, with working links, timestamps, hashtags and @handles.
- Original thumbnails instead of localized or test versions.
- The original audio track instead of a dub. Pick another track yourself and it stays picked.
- No subtitles switched on without asking.
- No AI-generated video summaries.

The popup shows what changed on the current page, with YouTube's version struck through next to the original. Every feature has its own switch.

No account, no setup, no tracking. restoreyt only talks to YouTube.

## Single purpose

Show YouTube content in the form its creator uploaded it, undoing automatic translation, dubbing and localization.
